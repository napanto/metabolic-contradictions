# ManConCorpus source and derived benchmark

`mancon_source.xml` is a **verbatim source copy**, included here beside the small derived
benchmark files for a self-contained private research checkout. It is not a new annotation
or a derived corpus. The original private inbox copy remains unchanged.

- Authors: Abdulaziz Alamri and Mark Stevenson, University of Sheffield.
- Paper: *A corpus of potentially contradictory research claims from cardiovascular
  research abstracts*, Journal of Biomedical Semantics 7:36 (2016),
  DOI `10.1186/s13326-016-0083-z`, PMID 27267226.
- Dataset source: https://staffwww.dcs.shef.ac.uk/people/M.Stevenson/resources/bio_contradictions/
- Acquired in the project paper inbox on 2026-11-11; copied here on 2026-11-12.
- SHA256: `c6925524b0117e877e64782af048d18a851bf0127361abe29587858238a180f4`.
- Dataset permission reported by the source: “freely available for use by researchers”.
  The dataset does not carry a formal licence in the downloaded package. The paper's
  CC BY 4.0 licence is **not assumed to license the separate dataset**. Retain attribution
  and this statement; this checkout is for the requested private educational project.
- Contents verified: 24 questions/reviews, 259 claims, 180 YS/79 NO, 165 CAUS/94 EVAL;
  1,775 within-question pairs, including 1,040 equal-type pairs.

`mancon_claims.jsonl`, `mancon_pairs.jsonl` and `mancon_split.json` are generated by
`python -m chl_rag.mancon prepare`. Stable claim IDs retain original XML order. Pair labels
mean disagreement of the original question-specific YS/NO assertions, not new judgments of
clinical truth. Question groups linked by repeated PMID or normalized exact claim text stay
in one split and form one bootstrap component. The split is 8 development questions and
16 test questions (7 and 15 independent components respectively).

Reading coverage: Alamri corpus construction, claim types, Tables 4–5, annotation agreement;
Xie 2024 pair construction, question/claim assertion method and Table 1. The existing paper
inbox contains both papers and their verified metadata. The Xie literature numbers are
stored separately in `results/mancon_literature_reference.json`; they are not held-out
measurements from this project's split.
