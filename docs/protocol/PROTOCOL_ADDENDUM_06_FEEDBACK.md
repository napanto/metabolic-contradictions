# Addendum06 operational correction: uncertainty feedback denominator

14 November2026, prepared during full-F extraction before classification or C. This refines the07:55 adaptive transport instruction and the signed31fb581 provider64 extension; it changes no scientific input or output.

The first adaptive run exposed a completion-order effect. Around08:27,42 new unknown requests out of approximately1679 observed POSTs were all480-second timeouts (about2.5% overall, approximately1.1–3.3% by provider). Fast results had already arrived; the remaining slow requests expired later. Their fraction among the last20 completions reached60% on one provider. Repeated reductions then reacted to the same requests admitted before the first reduction, taking several caps down to1–3 even though cumulative provider uncertainty remained below5%.

Use each provider’s cumulative observed-POST uncertainty fraction, `unknown_posts / total_posts`, for the rising-uncertainty feedback decision, after at least10 observations. Halve only when a new unknown event raises this fraction above5%, retaining the15-second halving cooldown. The existing last20-completion fraction remains a labelled diagnostic and does not control capacity. Known generation-free rejections remain known, not unknown charges. Provider counters continue across restart; they are not reset to hide earlier events.

All other controls from the07:55 extension remain: hard64/provider maximum, minimum1,256 extraction and320 classification workers; immediate429 reduction subject to cooldown; three5xx events within60seconds; clean60-second interval plus five successes restores eight slots; no cancellation of existing leases; bounded failed-slot probing; persistent-throttle reporting. Existing caps and counters are preserved at cutover and recover through the registered clean-interval rule, without a manual reset to64.

The cumulative rate reacts more slowly to a new isolated missing-cost/timeout cluster after a long clean history. This is an explicit operational tradeoff to avoid biased feedback from arrival order. Direct429 and5xx burst signals retain their immediate controls. Every unknown cost remains in the authoritative accounting audit, and the separate overall>5% reporting rule still applies. No response is relabelled, repaired or omitted, and no cost zero is invented.
